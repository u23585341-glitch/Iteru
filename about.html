ABOUT THE TOOLKIT

Learning through play.

This educational toolkit has been created to support engaging learning experiences for learners aged 10–12.

01 — THE BRIEF

Designing learning through play.

This interdisciplinary project between Information Design and Humanities Education at UP aims to create tangible, play-based educational resources for Social Sciences (History and Geography) in Grades 4–6 classrooms in South Africa.

Rather than simply making curriculum content visually appealing, the focus is on designing playful, human-centred learning interactions that help learners grasp abstract concepts such as time, continuity, cause and consequence, place, space, and interdependence.

The resources must be CAPS-aligned, ethically developed, and sensitive to diverse, multilingual, and unevenly resourced classroom contexts, avoiding stereotypes or superficial gamification.

Groups will research, prototype, and test solutions that support critical, age-appropriate engagement with history and geography, ensuring relevance, accountability, and sustainability.

Ultimately, the project emphasizes design as a social practice that fosters civic responsibility, cultural awareness, and children's development through experiential learning.

02 — OUR APPROACH

Ancient Egypt through active learning.

This toolkit is for Grade 5 Social Sciences and is aligned with the CAPS curriculum unit on The Way of Life in Ancient Egyptian Society.

Its purpose is to help learners gain a meaningful understanding of Ancient Egypt's people, social structures, and beliefs through active, hands-on learning.

The method of play-based learning helps learners to collaborate using problem-solving, role-play, construction, discovery, and hieroglyphics.

Learners further develop historical understanding, critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and teamwork.
